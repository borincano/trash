HOW IT WORKS

A short, intense, readable combat loop — built for touch screens and PC.

01

Enter the Command Hub (intro → access key → home).

02

Choose a mode: Career, Endless, Daily, Event, or Online VS.

03

Each wave lasts 60 seconds. Hit the score objective.

04

Every 10s threat rises: denser spawns, elites, bosses, enemy fire.

05

Between waves pick upgrades. In Garage unlock permanent ships and perks.

06

Online VS: create or join a room, fight 1v1 first-to-10k / 90s.
